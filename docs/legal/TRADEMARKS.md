# Trademarks and branding

The application source licence does not grant rights to third-party names, logos,
datasets or artwork beyond their own applicable licences and permissions. RankOffline's
name and visual identity should not be used to imply an official affiliation with a
catalog or cover provider.

An installed pack is responsible for declaring its publisher, data sources, licences,
notices and any bundled source logos. The Android engine reads those declarations
without hardcoding a provider's branding. Inclusion of an attribution in a pack is not
proof that a trademark or dataset may be redistributed; publication requires a separate
rights review.
