RankOffline is a free, open-source Android app for rating and ranking
anime, manga, movies, TV series, and books.

Install only the offline catalog packs you want. Browse and search your
installed catalogs without an account or an internet connection.

Features:
* Independently downloadable Anime, Manga, Movies, Series, and Books packs
* Simple ratings with an optional personal-taste bonus
* Detailed ratings with weighted categories and reusable rating profiles
* Personal ranking lists, category sorting, and a Top 3 podium
* Filters, alternative title searches, and optional grouping of related items
* Custom themes, selectable launcher icons, and multiple interface languages
* Optional cover downloads and locally stored notes

Your scores, notes, rating profiles and preferences stay on your device.
No registration, tracking, advertisements or cloud ratings database.

An internet connection is required only to download catalog packs and
optional cover images. The downloaded catalog data is stored locally.

Important: choosing to delete a pack together with its data also deletes
its local ratings, notes, covers, and associated settings.

Third-party catalogs and cover artwork are governed by their respective
providers' terms. RankOffline is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
