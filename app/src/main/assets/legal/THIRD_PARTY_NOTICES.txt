BUNDLED DATABASES AND METADATA SOURCES

AnimeAPI by nattadasu supplies the Anime catalog foundation and provider
mapping. AnimeAPI software is MIT-licensed; its compiled database is identified
as ODbL 1.0 with contents under DbCL 1.0, and some raw components are CC0 1.0.
AnimeAPI requires credit to AnimeAPI and Anime Offline Database.
https://github.com/nattadasu/animeApi

anime-offline-database supplies Anime title aliases only. The bundled derived
anime_aliases.jsonl.gz uses cedya77/anime-offline-database release 2026-42,
continuing the original manami-project/anime-offline-database project. The
database is ODbL 1.0 and its contents are DbCL 1.0.
https://github.com/cedya77/anime-offline-database
https://github.com/manami-project/anime-offline-database

AniList supplies selected Anime metadata, explicit relations, popularity, and
primary cover metadata. Attribution does not imply authorization for broader
collection or endorsement by AniList. Known collection and competing-service
restrictions remain unresolved and require provider confirmation for release.
https://anilist.co

Jikan is used only as an existing Anime cover fallback. Jikan is an unofficial
MyAnimeList API; neither Jikan nor MyAnimeList endorses RankOffline.
https://jikan.moe

MangaDex supplies Manga metadata, title aliases, tags, creator relationships,
variant relations, and cover references. RankOffline does not offer chapter
reading and does not distribute scanlation content. Current acceptable-use
requirements include service credit and must be reassessed before advertising
or paid service features are introduced.
https://mangadex.org

TMDB supplies Movie and Series metadata, collection/season grouping,
alternative titles, company/network data, and poster references.
Current use is non-commercial; commercial use requires separate review with
TMDB.
https://www.themoviedb.org

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Open Library supplies Work metadata and author records through offline bulk
dumps. Book covers are retrieved separately through the Open Library Covers
service. No claim is made that all cover images are CC0 or public domain.
https://openlibrary.org
